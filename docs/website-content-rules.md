# JonnyLab website content rules

## Brand position

JonnyLab is an everyday problem-solving product lab. Lead with the concrete problem a product solves. AI is a means, not the category. Privacy is a design and trust principle, not the company category.

## Product status

- **Available:** publicly obtainable and supported now. Name the verified platform.
- **Coming soon:** release preparation is active and the launch scope is confirmed.
- **In App Review:** the exact platform build has been submitted to its public app store but is not yet available.
- **Closed testing:** the platform build is limited to an invited test group and must not be described as publicly available.
- **In development:** an implementation exists, but is not publicly available.
- **Experimental:** a prototype or vertical slice exists and its scope may change.
- Ideas without an implementation stay in the private Product Brain and do not appear as products.

Review status labels whenever a store release, pause, rename, or material scope change occurs.
Always write platform states separately; one platform's release must not imply availability on another platform.

## Copy

- Explain what the product helps someone do before mentioning AI or privacy.
- Prefer short, factual sentences. Avoid “revolutionary,” “smartest,” and unsupported superlatives.
- Use “AI-powered” only when AI is material to the shipped experience.
- Never say “on-device,” “no uploads,” “no analytics,” or “no data collection” without checking the current implementation and third-party SDKs.
- Distinguish data handled by JonnyLab from data processed by Apple, Google, RevenueCat, AI providers, crash reporting, analytics, or hosting providers.

## Legal and trust content

- Keep a separate privacy policy and terms page for every released product whose data handling, purchases, accounts, or platform behavior differs.
- Update both English and Korean documents together.
- Verify permissions, telemetry, crash reporting, billing, account systems, cloud storage, AI endpoints, retention, deletion, and contact details before changing claims.
- Date policy changes when product behavior or third-party processing changes; do not change dates for brand-only edits.
- Mark questions requiring statutory interpretation or jurisdiction-specific drafting as **legal review recommended**.

## Metadata and navigation

- Keep page title, description, canonical URL, Open Graph, sitemap, visible status, and structured data consistent.
- Do not include an unreleased idea in structured product data.
- Product pages must link to current support, privacy, and terms pages.
