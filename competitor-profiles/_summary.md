# Competitive research summary

## Landscape

Okara represents the all-in-one AI marketing-operations category: shared strategy context, specialized agents, integrations, and recurring measurement. JonnyLab does not need the same breadth yet. The highest-leverage pieces are a reliable message source of truth and low-friction assets that help other people describe and share released products accurately.

## Decision

| Capability | JonnyLab decision | Reason |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Shared marketing context | Adopt now | Reduces status and message drift across agent-assisted work |
| Public creator/media assets | Adopt now | Removes friction from accurate coverage and sharing |
| SEO/GEO technical fixes | Continue selectively | Existing sitemap, metadata, `llms.txt`, and public Agent Skill provide a strong base |
| Automated social publishing | Defer | Requires stronger content operations and explicit approval controls |
| Mass content generation | Do not adopt now | Volume is not evidence of useful demand |
| Influencer campaign automation | Validate manually first | Fit and conversion should be proven before adding tooling or spend |
