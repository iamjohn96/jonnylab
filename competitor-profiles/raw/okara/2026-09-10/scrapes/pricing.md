# Okara pricing — raw research notes

- URL: https://okara.ai/pricing
- Captured: 2026-09-10
- Free plan: website analysis, a product information document, positioning/ICP messaging, and a 30-day marketing strategy.
- Paid tiers displayed: AI CMO Lite at $129 monthly and AI CMO Pro at $249 monthly; annual billing is also offered.
- Lite adds design and competitor documents, monthly content strategy, SEO/GEO, influencer, X, and coding agents.
- Pro adds Reddit distribution, long-form writing, LinkedIn, and UGC video capabilities.
- GitHub delivery is described as reviewable pull requests for SEO and GEO fixes.
