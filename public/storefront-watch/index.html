Why this workflow exists

A storefront can look live while its buying path is wrong.

An unexpected destination, changed Buy link, or inconclusive browser result can leave a team without a clear answer. A basic uptime signal cannot show whether the intended checkout entry is still reachable.

Current boundary

A product preview, not a live monitoring claim.

FAQ

What this preview can answer.

Does the demo place an order or process a payment?

No. It uses fictional routes and local records only.

Does it monitor a live storefront today?

No. This page demonstrates the controlled workflow. Live monitoring, verified alert delivery, billing, and production operations are not enabled.

What does “human-controlled recovery” mean here?

An inconclusive outcome is not treated as healthy or silently retried. It is recorded for authorized human review.
