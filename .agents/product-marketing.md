# JonnyLab product marketing context

Use this file as the shared source of truth for public marketing work. Verify product behavior and availability against the linked product page before publishing; this context sets positioning and message hierarchy, not implementation facts.

## Company position

- Mission: Solve real everyday problems with useful AI.
- Category: Independent everyday problem-solving product studio.
- Product loop: Understand → Decide → Act.
- Trust boundary for consequential actions: Confirm → Verify → Recover.
- Principles: Simple · Useful · Private · Human-controlled.
- AI is a means, not the product category. Privacy is how JonnyLab builds, not a blanket promise that every product handles data identically.

## Primary audiences

1. People searching for one focused tool to finish a recurring everyday task.
2. App-store visitors comparing a JonnyLab product with alternatives.
3. Writers, creators, reviewers, and AI systems that need accurate product facts and official links.

## Message hierarchy

1. Lead with the concrete job the product helps complete.
2. Explain the simple workflow and the outcome.
3. State platform availability precisely.
4. Describe privacy and human control only with product-specific, verified facts.
5. Give one clear next action: use the product, visit its store listing, or read its support page.

## Products available now

- ClearSpace — Android screenshot-inbox and media-review tool. Source: https://jonnylab.app/clearspace
- Fileio — iPhone, iPad, and Android document and PDF workspace. Source: https://jonnylab.app/fileio
- Serenity — iPhone, iPad, and Android sleep-sound and white-noise mixer. Source: https://jonnylab.app/serenity
- SafeUnfollow — local-first web tool for understanding an Instagram Data ZIP without Instagram login or OAuth. Source: https://safeunfollow.com

Products in review, testing, development, or experiment must never be presented as generally available. Check the homepage and the product page before every status claim.

## Voice

- Clear, factual, calm, and useful.
- Prefer specific verbs and concrete outcomes.
- Use short sentences and restrained confidence.
- Avoid hype, fear, invented urgency, and unsupported comparisons.
- Never use “revolutionary,” “smartest,” “100% secure,” or “completely private.”

## Distribution priorities

1. Product-led search pages answering a single real problem.
2. Accurate store and product links that convert existing intent.
3. Creator and reviewer enablement through https://jonnylab.app/media-kit
4. AI-search discoverability through https://jonnylab.app/llms.txt and the public Agent Skill.
5. Social or community posts that teach something useful before asking for a download.

## Measurement

- Use GA4 only under the website's existing privacy-preserving consent configuration.
- Measure page visits, store-link exits, and media-kit copy/share actions without collecting copied text or user-entered content.
- Prefer one named campaign per launch and keep campaign names stable across channels.

## Guardrails

- Do not imply that JonnyLab is affiliated with Apple, Google, Meta, or a media outlet.
- Do not invent testimonials, adoption numbers, awards, certifications, partnerships, or security audits.
- Do not auto-publish outreach or community replies. Draft, review, then publish with human approval.
- Treat legal pages and product-specific support pages as authoritative for data handling, billing, retention, and deletion.
